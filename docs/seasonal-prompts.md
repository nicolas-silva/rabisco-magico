# Rabisco Mágico — Seasonal drawing prompts (12 months)

One theme per month, with 2–3 prompt ideas each. These are **prompts
for the child to draw** — shown in the app as inspiration, never
sent to the AI as the image prompt. No AI call is involved in this
list; it is a static, human-written list.

Rules every prompt follows:

- Kid-friendly for ages 4–10: animals, food, weather, holidays,
  nature — nothing scary, nothing with text in the drawing.
- Draw-able with a finger: big shapes, few details, bold colours.
- One clear scene per prompt (the transform works best when the
  drawing has one subject and a background, not ten tiny things).
- No licensed characters, no brands, no real people.
- Southern-hemisphere seasons (most launch families are in
  Australia) with the northern-hemisphere season noted where it
  differs, so the list works in Brazil and elsewhere too.

Each month lists the theme in pt-BR and English (Spanish follows
the same theme; the app's locale files carry the UI strings).
Suggested colours are hints for the child, not requirements.

## January — Summer beach / Praia de verão

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma praia com sol, mar e um castelo de areia | Draw a beach with sun, sea and a sandcastle |
| Desenhe um sorvete gigante derretendo no sol | Draw a giant ice cream melting in the sun |
| Desenhe um peixinho nadando entre corais coloridos | Draw a little fish swimming among colourful corals |

Colours: yellow sun, blue sea, orange sand. Southern summer;
in the northern hemisphere, swap in "snowy beach day" only if
the child asks — the beach works anywhere.

## February — Carnival & celebration / Carnaval e festa

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma máscara de carnaval bem colorida | Draw a very colourful carnival mask |
| Desenhe confetes e balões caindo do céu | Draw confetti and balloons falling from the sky |
| Desenhe um desfile com um carro alegórico | Draw a parade with a decorated float |

Colours: every colour at once. Keep shapes big: masks and
balloons transform beautifully.

## March — Autumn leaves / Folhas de outono

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma árvore com folhas laranjas caindo | Draw a tree with orange leaves falling |
| Desenhe um ouriço (porco-espinho) numa pilha de folhas | Draw a hedgehog in a pile of leaves |
| Desenhe uma abóbora sorrindo no jardim | Draw a smiling pumpkin in the garden |

Colours: orange, red, brown, yellow. Southern autumn; in the
north this is spring — swap in "first flowers" (see September)
if it fits the child's season better.

## April — Rain & rainbows / Chuva e arco-íris

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe um arco-íris depois da chuva | Draw a rainbow after the rain |
| Desenhe um guarda-chuva amarelo numa poça d'água | Draw a yellow umbrella in a puddle |
| Desenhe um sapinho pulando na chuva | Draw a little frog jumping in the rain |

Colours: rainbow stripes, grey clouds, bright yellow umbrella.
Rain + sun in one drawing gives the AI a clear sky to build.

## May — Mother's Day flowers / Flores para a mamãe

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe um buquê de flores para alguém que você ama | Draw a bouquet of flowers for someone you love |
| Desenhe um jardim com abelhas e borboletas | Draw a garden with bees and butterflies |
| Desenhe um coração gigante feito de flores | Draw a giant heart made of flowers |

Colours: pink, purple, green. No names or text in the drawing —
the flowers say it.

## June — Winter snow / Neve de inverno

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe um boneco de neve com cachecol e cenoura | Draw a snowman with a scarf and a carrot nose |
| Desenhe um pinguim escorregando no gelo | Draw a penguin sliding on the ice |
| Desenhe uma casa quentinha com fumaça na chaminé | Draw a cosy house with smoke from the chimney |

Colours: white, light blue, red scarf. Southern winter; in
Brazil's June, festas juninas fit here too — see the note below.

> Festa junina variant (Brazil, June): draw a bonfire with
> colourful flags (bandeirinhas) and a corn cob — "Desenhe uma
> fogueira com bandeirinhas coloridas e um milho".

## July — Space adventure / Aventura no espaço

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe um foguete voando para a Lua | Draw a rocket flying to the Moon |
| Desenhe um astronauta flutuando entre as estrelas | Draw an astronaut floating among the stars |
| Desenhe um alienígena simpático no seu planeta | Draw a friendly alien on its planet |

Colours: dark blue sky, white stars, silver rocket. School
holidays in Australia — a good month for a "draw every day"
streak.

## August — Under the sea / Fundo do mar

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma baleia gigante soprando água | Draw a giant whale spouting water |
| Desenhe um polvo com oito braços coloridos | Draw an octopus with eight colourful arms |
| Desenhe um tesouro escondido no fundo do mar | Draw a hidden treasure at the bottom of the sea |

Colours: deep blue, turquoise, coral pink. Big sea creatures
fill the frame well.

## September — Spring garden / Jardim de primavera

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe as primeiras flores da primavera | Draw the first flowers of spring |
| Desenhe um passarinho construindo um ninho | Draw a little bird building a nest |
| Desenhe uma borboleta pousando numa flor | Draw a butterfly landing on a flower |

Colours: light green, pink, yellow. Southern spring; in the
north this is autumn — swap in the March leaf prompts.

## October — Halloween (gentle) / Dia das Bruxas (leve)

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma abóbora sorridente com uma vela dentro | Draw a smiling pumpkin with a candle inside |
| Desenhe um fantasminha simpático voando | Draw a friendly little ghost flying |
| Desenhe um gato preto sentado na Lua | Draw a black cat sitting on the Moon |

Colours: orange, purple, black. Friendly only — no scary
monsters, no blood, nothing that would trip the safety filters.

## November — Dinosaurs / Dinossauros

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe um dinossauro de pescoço comprido comendo folhas | Draw a long-necked dinosaur eating leaves |
| Desenhe um vulcão soltando fumaça com dinossauros por perto | Draw a volcano smoking with dinosaurs nearby |
| Desenhe um ovo de dinossauro rachando | Draw a dinosaur egg hatching |

Colours: green, brown, volcano red-orange. Dinosaurs are the
most-requested theme in testing with 4–7 year olds.

## December — Christmas & summer holidays / Natal e férias

| pt-BR | English |
|---|---|
| Desenhe uma árvore de Natal cheia de enfeites | Draw a Christmas tree full of ornaments |
| Desenhe o Papai Noel voando no trenó (ou de trenó na praia!) | Draw Santa flying in his sleigh (or on a beach sleigh!) |
| Desenhe uma ceia de Natal com um bolo gigante | Draw a Christmas dinner with a giant cake |

Colours: red, green, gold. In Australia Christmas is summer —
a Santa on the beach is a perfectly good drawing too.

## How to use this list

- Show **one prompt at a time** in the app ("Ideia de hoje"), not
  the whole list — choice overload kills drawing for 4-year-olds.
- Rotate weekly within the month; the same theme three ways keeps
  it fresh without a new theme every day.
- The prompt is inspiration only: if the child draws something
  else entirely, that is fine — the transform follows the drawing,
  not the prompt.
- New prompts need a native-speaker check in English and Spanish
  before they ship (see `locales/README.md`).
