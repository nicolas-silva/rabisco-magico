## Common problems (what support will say)

- **"Transformar didn't work":** check the connection, then try again
  — the drawing is kept, and a failed transform never uses quota.
  If it keeps failing, we check the account and the image provider.
- **"The picture doesn't look like my child's drawing":** that's the
  hardest part of the product. Tell us what was lost or added; it helps
  the benchmark. We don't re-run a transform for free on a paid account
  beyond the fair-use cap, but a failed or refused generation is never
  charged against quota.
- **"I can't log in":** use the password-reset link on the sign-in
  page; if the email never arrives, check spam, then email support from
  the account email.

## Refunds

### Family — monthly (A$7.99/month)
- Cancel any time in the app (Settings → Subscription → Cancel).
  The subscription stays active until the end of the paid month; we do
  not charge the next month.
- **Refund:** if you cancel within 7 days of the first payment and have
  used fewer than 10 transforms on that subscription, email support for
  a full refund of that month. After that, no partial-month refunds —
  you keep access until the period ends.

### Family — yearly (A$54.99/year)
- Cancel any time; access runs until the end of the paid year.
- **Refund:** within 30 days of the yearly payment, email support for a
  pro-rata refund for the unused full months, minus the transforms
  already used at the monthly rate. After 30 days, no refund — access
  continues until the year ends.

### Magic Pack (A$4.99 for 30 transforms)
- **Refund:** within 7 days of purchase, if fewer than 5 of the 30
  transforms have been used, email support for a full refund. After
  that, the pack is yours to keep — transforms never expire while the
  account is active.

### How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method via Stripe, usually
within 5–10 business days. We confirm by email when the refund is sent.

### If Stripe or the law says otherwise
If a refund is required by Australian consumer law, that overrides
anything above. If in doubt, email support — we would rather refund
than argue.

## Deletion requests

- **Delete one work or the whole gallery:** in the app, Gallery →
  select → Delete. It is deleted from our storage immediately.
- **Close the account and delete everything:** Settings → Close
  account. This deletes the account, gallery, drawings and generated
  images. We confirm by email when it is done.
- **By email:** write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the account email with
  the subject "Delete my account". We aim to complete it within [5
  business days — to be confirmed] and confirm by email.
- Deletion is real deletion: we do not keep a copy, and we do not
  restore deleted works.
